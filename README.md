# Synquerra

## Smart Vehicle & Asset Management Platform

Synquerra is a comprehensive fleet and asset management solution that gives you real-time visibility and control over your vehicles, equipment, and personnel from anywhere. Whether you manage a delivery fleet, construction equipment, or service vehicles, Synquerra puts critical operational insights at your fingertips.

### Key Features

**Real-Time Tracking & Monitoring**
- Live GPS tracking of all your assets with update intervals as frequent as every 5 seconds
- Historical route playback and geofencing capabilities
- Battery levels, temperature monitoring, and device health status

**Intelligent Alerting System**
- Customizable alerts for speeding, geofence breaches, harsh driving, and maintenance needs
- Real-time notifications via push, SMS, and email
- Smart alert deduplication to reduce noise

**Advanced Scheduling & Dispatch**
- Create and manage work schedules for drivers and equipment
- Real-time schedule adherence tracking
- Automated notifications for schedule deviations

**Personnel & Asset Management**
- Driver assignment and rotation tracking
- Equipment utilization analytics
- Maintenance scheduling and service history

**Secure Access Control**
- Role-based permissions for team members
- Secure authentication with biometric login options
- Audit trails for all system actions

### Who Is This For?

- **Fleet Managers** overseeing delivery, service, or transportation vehicles
- **Construction Companies** tracking heavy equipment and job site assets  
- **Field Service Organizations** managing technicians and service vehicles
- **Logistics & Supply Teams** monitoring shipments and warehouse equipment
- **Safety & Compliance Officers** ensuring regulatory adherence and incident prevention

### Getting Started

1. Install the Synquerra mobile app from your device's app store
2. Create your organizational account
3. Add your vehicles/assets using their unique device identifiers
4. Configure alerts, schedules, and team member access
5. Start monitoring in real-time from the dashboard

### Technical Requirements

- iOS 13.0+ or Android 8.0+
- Active data connection (cellular or WiFi)
- Compatible tracking devices (contact support for device compatibility list)
